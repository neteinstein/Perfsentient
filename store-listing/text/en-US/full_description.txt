Is your phone slow, hot or full of ads out of nowhere? Perfsentient finds the apps behind it.

Perfsentient watches how your phone behaves, all day, in the background. Every evening you get a simple health report with a score and the apps that misbehaved, with a plain-language explanation of what each one did.

WHAT IT CATCHES
• Pop-up ads: apps that take over the screen while you use something else
• After-call ads: full-screen ads that appear when a phone call ends
• Overlay abuse: apps that draw over other apps and stay busy in the background
• Hidden apps: active in the background but with no icon in your app drawer
• Always-running apps: services that keep the CPU awake and drain the battery
• Night owls: apps that wake up while your screen is off
• Background data hogs: apps that transfer lots of data when you are not looking
• Notification spam
• Risky access: accessibility services, device administrators, unrestricted battery use
• Storage hogs: very large caches
• Scam apps: installed from a link in a chat, look-alike names, remote-access apps installed during a call, and apps that can read your codes
• Phone security: no screen lock, old security updates, USB debugging left on

YOUR DAILY REPORT
• A health score from 0 to 100 and a clear verdict: good, fair or poor
• Phone vitals: battery drain, screen-off drain, temperature and memory pressure
• A 7-day trend, so you can see if things are getting better
• Battery vs time open: the apps that used the most battery in the last 7 days, next to how long you had them open (estimated)
• Tap any app to see exactly what it did and open its App info to uninstall or restrict it

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
• The Apps tab lists every app flagged now or in the past
• Trust the apps you know are fine: they are left out of your reports and score, shown in another colour, and you can change your mind any time

PARENTAL MODE
Setting up a phone for a child? Turn on Parental mode in Settings and enter their age. A Parental help tab then lists the installed apps that child-safety organisations, regulators and investigations warn about for that age:
• Games and avatar worlds with open chat, where adults have groomed children
• Messaging and social apps named in sextortion warnings
• Random video chat, adult dating and anonymous Q&A apps
• AI companions, live streaming with paid gifts, secret vaults that hide photos or apps, and gambling
Each warning explains why the app is flagged, shows the suggested and minimum age, and tells you what you can do. Mark an app as reviewed once you have set it up together. Parental mode needs no extra permission, and the age you enter never leaves the phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything is analysed on your phone
• The app has no internet permission at all, so it cannot send your data anywhere
• No account, no ads, no analytics
• Notifications are only counted per app. Their content is never read

HOW IT WORKS
Turn on Usage access and Notification access in Android settings: the Settings tab explains each one and opens the right screen. A light check runs every 15 minutes, and your report arrives at the time you choose.

AVAILABLE IN
English, Portuguese, French, Spanish, Italian, Romanian and German. On Android 13 and later you can choose the app's language in Android settings.

Perfsentient does not remove apps for you. It tells you which ones to look at, and why.
